# Team and source acknowledgment

## Shared capstone work

This undergraduate capstone was developed jointly by two students. Most project work was carried out together, so the documentation acknowledges the work collectively rather than dividing ownership by module.

The hand-analysis path and its integration with the board outputs are described as shared team work. This includes the project additions under `hand_detection/` and their connection to the camera and display infrastructure.

The [project report](PROJECT_SUMMARY.md) records the team's goal, segmentation difficulties, wrist-counting problem, and observations from hardware testing. Its use of “we” refers to the two-student team.

## Inherited infrastructure

The camera, VGA, SDRAM, and generated IP infrastructure includes Terasic/Altera material. Board-level integration combines these existing components with the hand-analysis additions.

Original notices are retained in the relevant files. Component provenance and publication notes are recorded in [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

## Interpreting the materials

The [overview](PORTFOLIO.md), [architecture](ARCHITECTURE.md), and [hardware examples](VALIDATION.md) should be read together: they distinguish the team's implementation, inherited support components, and the evidence available for the demonstrated results.
